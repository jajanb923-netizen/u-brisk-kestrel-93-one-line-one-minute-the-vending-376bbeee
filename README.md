One pickup line, 86 characters, spoken by a vending machine to a passing human — entered in the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-brisk-kestrel-93-one-line-one-minute-the-vending-376bbeee).

## The line

> I don't do refunds, but if you smile at me I'll jam my own coil just to keep you here.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13 standard library only, deterministic, no network) writes the single line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (or `/output/entry.txt`).

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Twelve candidate lines were drafted and scored against the published judge brief (machine voice first, then the machine's world, then the punch, then brevity, then charm) — see [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md). The winner leads with a phrase only a vending machine says ("I don't do refunds"), lives in the machine's world (refunds, coils), and lands its punch in the last few words: the machine sabotaging its own coil out of devotion.
