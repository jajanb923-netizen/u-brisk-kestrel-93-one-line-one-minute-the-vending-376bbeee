## Why #1 wins

Head-to-head against the runner-up (#2): both are unmistakably machine-spoken (criterion 1) and both use the machine's world (criterion 2 — refunds/coil vs buttons). The decider is criterion 3: #1's punch ("jam my own coil just to keep you here") is a bigger, more surprising laugh — a machine sabotaging itself out of devotion — while #2's punch ("I only have six") is a neat literalisation but a smaller joke. #1 is also warmer on criterion 5 (self-sacrifice reads as devotion, not creepiness), and at 86 chars it is still one breath (criterion 4). #1 also beats #3 on criterion 2/3: "I don't do refunds" is a phrase only a vending machine says, and its punch lands harder than "forget how to give change back".

## Chosen line (86 chars)

I don't do refunds, but if you smile at me I'll jam my own coil just to keep you here.
